Class 10 Physics - Past Paper (Electricity and Circuits)

Q1. Calculate the electric current when 120 coulombs of charge pass a point in 60 seconds. (2 marks)
Q2. State the SI unit of electric current and name the instrument used to measure it. (2 marks)
Q3. A resistor has 6 volts across it and carries 2 amperes. Calculate its resistance using Ohm's law. (2 marks)
Q4. At fixed voltage and constant temperature, what happens to the current if the resistance is doubled? Explain using Ohm's law. (3 marks)
Q5. Compare the resistances of a long thin wire and a short thick wire made from the same material at the same temperature. (3 marks)
Q6. Calculate the equivalent resistance of 2 ohm and 4 ohm resistors in series. State how the current compares through them. (3 marks)
Q7. Calculate the equivalent resistance of two 6 ohm resistors connected in parallel. (2 marks)
Q8. Explain why household appliances are connected in parallel rather than in series. (3 marks)
Q9. Calculate the electrical power of a component with 12 volts across it and a current of 2 amperes. (2 marks)
Q10. Calculate the energy transferred by a 1 kilowatt heater in 2 hours, in both kilowatt-hours and joules. (3 marks)
